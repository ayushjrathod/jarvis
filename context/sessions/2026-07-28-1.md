## The find

The session-20 spike concluded window management needed a GNOME shell
extension. It had tested `Shell.Eval` (locked since GNOME 41) and
`Shell.Introspect` (AccessDenied) — and stopped. **AT-SPI2 was never tried.**
Measured today: 9 apps on the a11y bus, window titles readable, and app windows
carrying `Component` (geometry, `grabFocus`) and **`Action` (`doAction`)** —
i.e. click a widget by accessible name, no coordinates. That is the deferred
capability *and* the semantic-clicking tier the Hermes audit ranked highest.

Two caveats measured alongside it: `toolkit-accessibility` is `false` (so
Chromium/Electron apps are largely absent from the tree until the
`org.a11y.Status` flags are set — the same thing cua's driver does at startup),
and the tree is messy (Nautilus reports a frame titled "Telegram Desktop").
